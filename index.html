📈 Plant Growth Experiment

How does light affect plant growth over 20 days?

🔬 Observation Station

Look at Day 5! The plant in the darkness actually shot up faster at first. Why?

Explanation: Plants in darkness quickly stretch out, desperately trying to find sunlight. However, without light for photosynthesis, they eventually run out of stored energy and stop growing. The plant in sunlight grew steadily because it could make its own food, becoming taller and healthier by Day 20!
